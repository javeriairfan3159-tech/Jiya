Questions

FAQ

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation envelope is the true count. If a name is listed, they are welcome. Message us if something has changed.

Are children invited?

Yes — nieces, nephews, and little cousins have a seat. We will have an early supper plate and a quiet room after speeches.

What should I wear?

Garden formal. Earth tones and linen love this landscape. The ceremony is outdoors on gravel.

Will there be parking?

A small lot sits below the villa. We strongly prefer the Pienza shuttle so the white road stays clear.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, with the barn as weather cover. Tuscany in October is usually gold and mild; we still have a plan B.

How do I RSVP?

Use the RSVP page by 1 September 2026. One form per household is perfect.
