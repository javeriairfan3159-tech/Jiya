## Full description — paste as-is

```
Plan the trip. Enjoy the trip.

Jiya is an all-in-one travel planner app for your next vacation, weekend getaway, or long-haul adventure. Explore places, organize trips, track travel expenses, save notes, and ask a built-in Travel AI Assistant — in one clean app.

Whether you are booking a solo escape, a couple’s holiday, or a trip with friends, Jiya keeps destinations, budget, and ideas together so you can stop switching between notes, chats, and spreadsheets.


EXPLORE PLACES
Discover beaches, mountains, attractions, and restaurants in one feed. Filter by type, save the spots you love, and tap Check Location before you go. From Bondi Beach to Mount Fuji, the Colosseum to the Eiffel Tower — find your next destination and build the trip around it.

MY TRIPS
Keep every journey on a simple home screen. Jump into Money, Notes, and Explore from Essential Tools, and see your trips at a glance. Start a new journey whenever inspiration hits.

WALLET & EXPENSES
Stay on budget while you travel. See total spent against your trip budget with a live progress ring, add expenses in one tap, split the bill with friends, and convert currency on the go. Know what you have used — and what is left — before the next dinner or train ticket.

TRAVEL AI ASSISTANT
Have a travel question? Ask the in-app AI assistant and get instant answers for your journey — packing tips, best seasons to visit, and on-the-road help. Less guesswork. More confidence.

NOTES & REMINDERS
Capture travel ideas, memories, and to-dos so nothing gets lost mid-trip. Keep reminders next to your notes when you need a nudge.

PROFILE & BADGES
Build your traveler profile, track trips and places, and collect badges as you go. Made for adventure enthusiasts who actually leave home.


WHY TRAVELERS USE JIYA
• One travel planner instead of five scattered apps
• Trip organizer + destination explore in the same place
• Travel budget tracker with split bill and currency converter
• AI travel helper when you are stuck on what to do next
• Clean, modern design that is fast to use on the road


PERFECT FOR
Solo travelers • Couples • Friends sharing costs • Weekend trips • Long vacations • Anyone who wants a simple trip planner, itinerary companion, and expense tracker in their pocket


HOW TO START
1. Download Jiya
2. Open Explore or start a journey
3. Save places, log expenses, and ask the Travel AI Assistant
4. Travel with a clearer plan — and a clearer budget


Jiya Travel Planner — plan smarter, spend wiser, go farther.

Questions or feedback? We read every message and keep improving the app for real trips, not just wish lists.
```
