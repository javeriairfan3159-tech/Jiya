## What you received

6 website pages:

5. RSVP
6. Info (wedding party, gallery, registry, FAQ)

## Edit the site

1. Open the template link and click **Use template**.
2. Click any text to change names, date, venue, and copy.
3. Click any photo → **Replace** → upload your engagement or venue images.
4. Update hotel and registry buttons: highlight the button text → link icon → paste your URL.
5. RSVP: either keep the on-page form and connect it in Canva, or replace the Send RSVP button with a link to Google Forms, Zola, or The Knot.

## Publish for guests

1. In Canva, open the website → **Publish** (or **Share** → **Website**).
2. Use the free Canva domain, or connect a custom domain if you have one.
3. Copy the live URL into your invitation, QR code, and Instagram.

## Mobile tip

Canva websites reflow on phones. Avoid dragging elements far off the original layout if you want the mobile view to stay clean. Duplicate a page before large experiments.
