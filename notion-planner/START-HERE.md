# ☕ Welcome to Your All-in-One Life Planner

> A calm space to plan your days, reach your goals, and keep life organized.

## Your planning system

| Area | What it helps you manage |
|---|---|
| 🗓️ Plan | Daily priorities, tasks, projects, and goals |
| 🌿 Grow | Habits, routines, reflection, and wellness |
| 💰 Organize | Spending, budgets, meals, and home life |
| ✨ Create | Content ideas, publishing, and creative work |

## Set up in 15 minutes

1. Import `DASHBOARD.md` into Notion.
2. Import every CSV inside the `databases` folder as its own database.
3. Drag the imported databases beneath your Dashboard in the sidebar.
4. Follow `SETUP-GUIDE.md` to set property types and create the recommended views.
5. Delete the sample rows when you are ready to begin.

## A simple weekly rhythm

- **Morning:** open Today, choose three priorities, and check your habits.
- **Evening:** clear completed tasks and write one line in the Daily Journal.
- **Sunday:** review goals, plan meals, schedule content, and reset the week.
- **Month end:** review spending, progress, and lessons before planning again.

## Personalize your planner

Suggested minimal beige palette:

- Cream — `#F7F1E8`
- Sand — `#DECDB8`
- Mocha — `#8B6F5A`
- Sage — `#A8B5A2`

Add your own cover photos, update the icons, rename sections, and hide any area
you do not need. The best planner is the one you enjoy opening.
