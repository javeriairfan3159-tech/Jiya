Use this if you want an editable Canva listing. Recreate the layout on a 5×7 in canvas, then duplicate for the other sizes. Do not screenshot a competitor template.

## Layout recipe (5×7)

1. Ivory paper with a faint blush wash top-left and sky wash bottom-right.
2. Blush peony cascade hugging the left edge; powder-blue hydrangea cascade on the right.
3. Double gold frame: 2 px gold, 11 px gap, 1 px champagne. Small rotated squares at the four corners.
4. Centered stack:
   - `A SWEET SURPRISE AWAITS` — Cinzel, 11 pt, gold, tracking 400+
   - gold hairline
   - `He` (sky script) · small gold `or` · `She` (blush script)
   - balloon pair with heart ribbons
   - `JOIN US FOR A` — Cinzel small caps
   - `Gender Reveal` — Great Vibes, 72–80 pt, blush deep
   - `BABY SHOWER` — Cinzel, sky deep, tracking 300+
   - `celebrating` italic + family name in Cinzel
   - Three columns: DATE / TIME / PLACE between champagne rules
   - RSVP line
   - Footer script: `we can’t wait to meet you`

## Elements to keep original

- Split botanicals (peony left, hydrangea right) — not footprints
- Balloon pair with heart-shaped ribbons — not glitter confetti
- Champagne foil frame — not a clipart ribbon banner
- One headline, not “WHAT WILL BABY BE?” twice
- No copied corner-confetti + gold-star scatter from another listing

## Upload path for a Canva product

1. Build the 5×7 as the master.
2. Duplicate pages for details, welcome, tag, books, raffle, and phone.
3. Mark every name/date/address text box as editable.
4. Export a PDF with a Canva template link for the Etsy download.
