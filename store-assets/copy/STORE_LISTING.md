## Full description (English)

Kite Master is the dark, neon utility hub for kite flyers — from rooftop launches to full pech battles.

Know the wind before you put a kite in the sky. Get live flying conditions, wind alerts, and a daily safety tip so every session starts smart.

**Live flying conditions**
See when the sky is perfect. Track wind speed at a glance, open a live forecast, and turn on wind alerts so you never miss a flyable window.

**Masterclass guides**
Learn the real techniques:
• How to launch a kite — wind direction, lift, and a clean takeoff
• Mastering the Pech — pull, slacken (dheeli chorne), and cut with control
• Manjha preparation — traditional coating methods, taught with safety first

**Safety & Knots Hub**
Metallic wire, chemical strings, and unsafe manjha can seriously hurt people. Kite Master puts hazard awareness up front, plus step-by-step emergency knots for bridle and string.

**Utility toolbox**
• Gallery of kite designs
• Live wind tracker
• Safety guidelines
• Daily kite tips (power lines, crowded rooftops, and more)

**Stay on a flying streak**
Build a daily streak, unlock badges like Starter, Wind Pro, and Knot Master, and keep showing up for the sky.

Whether you fly for Basant, for the neighbourhood pech, or just for the pull of the string — fly smarter, fight better, and come home safe.

Kite Master. The sky is calling.
