2018 · Rome

A wrong tram, a right person

Elena boarded the number eight meaning to go to Trastevere. Matteo was already on it, holding a paper bag of peaches and a novel with the spine cracked in half.

They both got off two stops too late, laughed about it on the platform, and walked until the peaches were gone. That is the whole beginning.
