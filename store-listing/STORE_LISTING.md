### Full description
```
V380 Pro Guide is the unofficial setup and troubleshooting companion for V380 Pro smart cameras. Pair a new camera, format a MicroSD card, turn on cloud backup, update firmware safely, or recover a frozen device — without digging through scattered videos.

WHAT YOU GET

• Interactive setup modules
Step-by-step help for Wi-Fi pairing, MicroSD formatting, recording modes, and recovery. Every guide shows difficulty and time so you know what you are starting.

• Three pairing methods
AP Hotspot Direct Connect (works offline), Wi-Fi Smart-Link on 2.4 GHz, and QR Code pairing. Bookmark the method that works in your home.

• Storage and recording
Format cards the right way (FAT32 vs exFAT), choose 24/7 loop or motion recording, and set up cloud backup for event clips.

• Firmware and unbrick recovery
Safe over-the-air (OTA) updates, plus advanced hard-reset steps for a solid red LED, a frozen camera, or a bricked device.

• Error solver
An interactive decision-tree diagnoser and a searchable V380 error-code dictionary (including codes such as 10001), plus audio/video fine-tuning tips.

• Smart tools
Network IP scanner, Wi-Fi signal meter, RTSP URL generator, and IR night-vision glare help when the live view looks washed out.

• PC, NVR, and sharing
Windows/macOS CMS setup, multi-user sharing permissions, and Hikvision/Dahua ONVIF NVR integration.

SEARCH AND SAVE
Search pairing, SD format, recovery, or an error code. Filter by Camera Pairing, Storage Settings, or Firmware & Recovery. Star the guides you need again.

WHO IT IS FOR
Home owners, shops, and installers who use V380 Pro cameras and want clear instructions instead of trial and error.

IMPORTANT
This is an unofficial guide and utility companion. It is not the official V380 / V380 Pro camera app and is not affiliated with the camera manufacturer. You still need the official camera app to view live video. Always follow on-device warnings before a reset or firmware update. Features described here depend on your camera model and firmware.
```
