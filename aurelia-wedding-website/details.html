RSVP

Saturday 18 October

The wedding day

Ceremony, aperitivo, supper, and dancing — all on the grounds of Villa Oliveto. Edit times, places, and dress notes to match your own weekend.

Order of the day

Schedule

3:30 pm

Guests arrive

Lemon courtyard. A cool drink, a shaded seat, and time to find your people.

4:00 pm

Ceremony

Under the olive trees, with the valley behind us. The ceremony is about twenty minutes.

5:00 pm

Aperitivo

Spritz, crostini, and the terrace. Photographs will happen quietly in the garden — stay, talk, eat.

7:00 pm

Dinner

Long tables in the barn. Family-style Tuscan courses, with vegetarian plates marked at the RSVP.

9:00 pm

Dancing

Courtyard lights, a small band, and a last song we will not name until the night.

Dress code

Garden formal

Linen, silk, and earth tones. Jackets welcome, ties optional. The ground is gravel and grass — choose a heel you can walk in, or a beautiful flat.

On the table

Dinner notes

Antipasti, handmade pasta, roast, and a citrus tart. Tell us allergies on the RSVP. Children are welcome; a quieter supper room is available after speeches.
