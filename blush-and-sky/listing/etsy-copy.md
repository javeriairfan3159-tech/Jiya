## Description

A sweet surprise awaits.

This Blush & Sky suite is a full gender-reveal baby shower set, not a one-page invite. Dusty rose peonies meet powder-blue hydrangeas on ivory paper, with a watercolor balloon pair whose ribbons form a heart — and room to drop in any family name.

WHAT YOU GET
• 5×7 print invitation
• Phone / evite (1080×1920)
• Matching details card (timeline, dress, games, registry)
• Welcome sign
• Round favor tag
• Books for baby insert
• Diaper raffle ticket
• High-resolution PNGs ready to print or text

EASY TO PERSONALIZE
Change the name, date, time, address, and RSVP line in the design files. Keep the script + serif pairing so the set still looks like one brand.

PERFECT FOR
Gender reveal showers, boy-or-girl parties, combined reveal-and-shower afternoons, and any “what will baby be?” gathering.

HOW TO USE
1. Open the design file you want to edit.
2. Swap in your party details.
3. Export PNG or PDF.
4. Print on 5×7 cardstock, or send the phone size as an evite.

PLEASE NOTE
This is a digital suite. No physical item is shipped. Mockup photos are examples — your files are the flat designs. Colors can shift slightly between screens and printers.
