# YouTube avatar script — Travel Planner (1–2 min)

Do **not** say the name Jiya. App name in this video: **Travel Planner**.

Spoken pace: ~140 words/minute. This script is **~220 words → about 1 minute 35 seconds**.

---

## Script (read this)

```
Planning a trip shouldn’t mean five different apps, messy notes, and a budget you only check when the holiday is over.

Travel Planner puts everything in one place — so you can plan the trip, and actually enjoy it.

Start with Explore Places. Browse beaches, mountains, attractions, and restaurants. Save the spots you love, and tap Check Location before you go. From a beach day to a mountain view, your next destination is already in the app.

Then open My Trips. Your journeys live on one home screen, with essential tools right there: Money, Notes, and Explore. New trip? One tap. You’re in.

Need to watch your spending? Wallet and Expenses shows what you’ve used against your trip budget. Add an expense in seconds. Split the bill with friends. Convert currency while you’re still at the café. No more guessing what you can still spend.

Stuck on a travel question? Ask the Travel AI Assistant. Packing tips, best time to visit, what to do next — instant answers, right in the app.

And when an idea hits you mid-flight or at 2 a.m., Notes and Reminders keep it. Capture memories, lists, and little reminders so nothing gets lost.

Your profile even tracks trips, places, and badges as you go — made for people who actually leave home.

Download Travel Planner. Explore more. Spend smarter. Travel better.
```

## Avatar tips

- Smile on the first and last line.
- Pause half a second after each feature name (Explore Places, Wallet and Expenses, Travel AI Assistant).
- Don’t rush the last line — that’s the call to action.
- If the avatar tool has a “friendly / warm” voice, use that, not “news anchor.”
